# ADR 007 — Effective-dated regulatory evidence

2026-10-21 (America/Los_Angeles). Preserve existing services and source-reference records. Add a provider-neutral effective-rule model, application provider port and reviewed California adapter. No framework or California-specific branches in the new domain evaluator. Jurisdiction and method identifiers are extensible strings; current API accepts shore-angling and hand-gathering in US-CA.

A rule records authority, source, species, jurisdiction, geographic scope, methods, legal start/end, whether expiration is known, reviewed date, review deadline, supported evidence dates, exceptions and structured restrictions. A null end with expirationKnown=true means no stated expiry, not a fabricated expiration. Legal effect, retrieval, review and supported coverage are separate concepts.

Scope evidence must be authoritative for the rule's geographic level. Canonical California State Parks entries establish the state scope only. They do not establish legal MPA or local-rule boundaries. Advisory MPA polygons never satisfy that legal-scope gate. A future geography adapter must explicitly supply authoritative scope identifiers, not use an arbitrary bounding box as legal evidence.

Evaluate matching species/method/jurisdiction only. Missing dates, unknown expiration, unresolved exceptions, stale review, unsupported activity dates or unresolved geography produce UNVERIFIED findings. Closure endpoints are inclusive; recurring seasons may cross year boundaries. Same-question contradictions remain unresolved. An independent verified prohibition can reject an activity despite other missing checks. Permission requires evidence for every existing regulatory question. Conditional limits cannot alone imply overall permission. Original evaluateRegulations export remains for compatibility; production location details use assessRegulations through the new application port.

Initial real data: CCR 28.00 California grunion closure and daily bag/possession limits. Effective 2022-06-01, no stated expiry. Supported reviewed activity window 2026-01-01 through 2026-10-21. Review due 2026-10-28 is an internal conservative seven-day maintenance deadline, not a legal expiry or promise of currentness. Future dates outside the reviewed window remain UNVERIFIED even before that deadline. No scheduled auto-renewal; a human must recheck official sources and amendments before updating coverage. Source availability checks do not establish absence of all emergency restrictions.

Manual verification process: inspect FGC 2021 rulemaking's California Grunion entry, its approved-language PDF (including redline rendering), current CDFW grunion guidance, current booklet and in-season index. Legal effective date comes from FGC adoption record, never the booklet publication or retrieval date. The PDF's deleted text must not be interpreted as operative law. Source references and current HTTP/content/hash evidence are in regulatory-delivery.md and regulatory-sources-live.log.

No production rule grants full fishing permission. No verified method/size/license/local/MPA/emergency clearance exists yet. Hand-only grunion guidance is displayed as a limitation, not encoded with an invented effective date. Surfperch regional differences and shore exceptions were researched but not automatically mapped from advisory polygons or supplied with invented commencement dates. Unit fixtures that demonstrate permitted/conditional states are not production regulations.
